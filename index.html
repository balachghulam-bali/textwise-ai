What is TextWise AI?

TextWise AI is a free, high-speed text utility web application designed to help writers, students, developers, and professionals instantly refine their content. Powered by Google Gemini AI, TextWise AI delivers instant grammar corrections, academic rewrites, bullet-point summaries, and key takeaway extractions in seconds.

Key Features

  • Grammar & Style Fixer: Instantly remove spelling and grammatical errors while enhancing tone.
  • Text Summarizer: Condense long articles, reports, or notes into brief, readable takeaways.
  • Academic Polish: Rewrite informal drafts into formal, professional academic writing.
  • Action Items Extractor: Turn long emails or documents into clear actionable bullet points.

How to Use TextWise AI

  1. Click the API Key button at the top right and enter your free Google Gemini API key.
  2. Paste your paragraph, draft, or article into the text area.
  3. Select your desired AI Action from the dropdown menu.
  4. Click Generate Output to get clean AI-processed text immediately.

🔒 Complete Privacy Guaranteed

Your API key and pasted text are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage. Your inputs are sent directly to Google Gemini's API without going through any third-party databases or servers.
